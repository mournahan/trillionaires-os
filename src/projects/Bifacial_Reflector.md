# Project: Bifacial Solar Reflector Shield
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Off-Grid & Power

## Overview
An active tracking albedo reflector for vertically mounted bifacial solar fences.

## The Concept
John's Indepwr bifacial panels capture light from both sides, especially snow-reflection in the winter. This project adds a motorized, highly polished aluminum shield at the base of the array. Using a simple ESP32 and light sensors, it actively tilts to bounce the maximum amount of ambient or snow-reflected light directly into the backside of the panels throughout the day, massively boosting winter yield without adding more costly panels.
