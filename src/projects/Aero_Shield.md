# Project: Aero-Shield Cabin Filtration
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Automotive & Health

## The Problem
During severe forest fire seasons, driving (or sheltering) in a vehicle exposes occupants to hazardous PM2.5 smoke and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs), which standard cabin air filters cannot stop.

## The Solution
A heavy-duty, military-grade air scrubbing system designed as a direct retrofit for the Jeep Gladiator and Wrangler HVAC intake. 
- Uses a multi-stage true HEPA and activated carbon filtration matrix. 
- Features a built-in ESP32 air-quality sensor that dynamically adjusts blower speed based on external smoke density. 
- **Impact**: Provides a perfectly pure, breathable safe-haven inside the vehicle during extreme environmental disasters.
