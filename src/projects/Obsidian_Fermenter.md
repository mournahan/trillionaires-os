# Project: Obsidian Fermentation Vault
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: High-End Culinary Tech

## Concept
A bespoke, luxury countertop appliance designed specifically for the health-conscious, scratch-cooking demographic. It automates the exact environmental conditions required for making artisanal plant-based milks, probiotic yogurts, sauerkraut, and Epsom-salt curdled tofu.

## The Hardware
- **Aesthetic**: Matte-black, brutalist/minimalist design. No flashy LEDs, just a subtle, hidden OLED display embedded in the glass that only wakes up when touched.
- **Power**: Native 48V DC option, designed to plug directly into off-grid solar architectures (like the EG4 system) without parasitic AC inverter losses.
- **Functionality**:
  - Precision heating/cooling curves to maximize specific probiotic strain developments.
  - Built-in pH monitoring probes to alert the user the exact moment the sauerkraut hits the optimal acidity for gut-health.
  - Automated pressing mechanism for tofu processing.
