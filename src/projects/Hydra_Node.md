# Project: Hydra-Node Purifier
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Humanitarian & Ecological

## The Problem: Microplastics & Forever Chemicals
Rural, off-grid, and disaster-stricken areas rely on water sources that are increasingly contaminated with microplastics, heavy metals, and PFAS (forever chemicals). Standard physical filters clog quickly and cannot catch molecular-level contamination.

## The Solution: Solar Electrocoagulation
A ruggedized, off-grid water purification node that utilizes **Electrocoagulation**. 
- It uses John's expertise in 48V DC solar architectures to run a direct electrical current through sacrificial aluminum or iron plates submerged in a water tank.
- The electrical current physically breaks the emulsion of the water, neutralizing the charge of contaminants. Microplastics, heavy metals, and forever chemicals instantly bind together into a visible floc (sludge) that floats to the top for easy skimming.
- **Impact**: It requires zero moving parts, no filter cartridges to replace, and scales infinitely based on solar yield. It can provide completely sterilized, heavy-metal-free water for a homestead or a disaster relief camp indefinitely.
