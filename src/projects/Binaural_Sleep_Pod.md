# Project: Binaural Sleep Pod
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Luxury Health

## Overview
A minimalist, EMF-blocking bed canopy integrated with acoustic therapy.

## The Concept
Perfect deep sleep is critical for longevity. This canopy acts as a highly aesthetic Faraday cage, blocking all ambient EMF (Wi-Fi, cellular) from reaching the sleeper. Simultaneously, it connects to a grid of bone-conduction transducers embedded beneath the mattress. It silently pulses Theta binaural frequencies directly through the sleeper's skeleton, locking the brain into deep REM and restorative sleep cycles without the need for headphones.
