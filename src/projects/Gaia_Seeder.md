# Project: Gaia Soil Seeder
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Ecological Restoration

## The Problem
After massive forest fires, the soil is sterilized and prone to extreme erosion and mudslides before native plants can recover.

## The Solution
A Jeep-towed or Drone-mounted dispensing mechanism utilizing a **Piston Pump and Static Mixer**.
- The static mixer dynamically combines native seeds (like white clover) with a nutrient-dense biochar and clay slurry.
- It rapidly dispenses thousands of perfectly encapsulated "seed bombs" across burn scars.
- **Impact**: The clay slurry protects the seeds from birds and UV light until the first rain, allowing for the rapid, automated re-seeding and stabilization of disaster zones.
