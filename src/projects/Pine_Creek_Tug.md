# Project: Pine Creek Autonomous Tug
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Logistics & Safety

## Overview
A 48V DC, AI-powered motorized pallet jack designed for the Pine Creek Pack facility.

## The Concept
Workplace injuries in packaging and logistics often stem from moving heavy pallets. This compact robotic tug hooks into standard pallet jacks. Using an ESP32 combined with Lidar and machine vision, it autonomously tows heavy loads across the warehouse floor, avoiding workers and obstacles, drastically reducing physical strain and improving operational efficiency.
