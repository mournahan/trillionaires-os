# Project: Kestrel Bio-Telemetry Anklet
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Ecological Hardware

## Concept
American Kestrels are small, requiring extremely lightweight husbandry gear. This project aims to design a micro-weight, hyper-advanced telemetry anklet for rehabilitation and flight training.

## Hardware Design
- **Material**: 3D-printed carbon fiber or aerospace-grade titanium for maximum strength-to-weight ratio. Sub-5 gram total payload.
- **Sensors**: 
  - Micro-IMU (Accelerometer/Gyroscope) to track exact dive speeds, wingbeat frequency, and G-forces.
  - Optical heart-rate sensor resting against the skin to monitor the raptor's stress levels during training.
- **Telemetry**: BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) or ultra-light LoRa module to beam the data back to a phone/dashboard in real-time.
- **Application**: Allows the falconer to know exactly when the bird is fatigued, stressed, or hitting optimal flight metrics before visual signs are apparent.
