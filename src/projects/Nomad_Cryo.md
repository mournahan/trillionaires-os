# Project: Nomad Cryo-Chiller
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Luxury Wellness

## The Problem
Cold plunge and cryotherapy are incredible for reducing inflammation and increasing longevity, but current commercial chillers require 120V/240V AC power, making them impossible for off-grid homesteads.

## The Solution
A ruggedized, 48V DC native chilling unit.
- Plugs directly into an off-grid solar battery bank without the massive parasitic loss of an AC inverter.
- Uses John's logistics and cold-chain expertise to maximize thermal efficiency using vacuum-insulated panels.
- **Impact**: Brings elite-tier sports medicine and longevity practices directly to off-grid and remote environments.
