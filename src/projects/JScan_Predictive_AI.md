# Project: JScan Predictive Diagnostics AI
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Automotive & Software

## Overview
A software layer that acts as a predictive AI mechanic for the Jeeps.

## The Concept
Standard JScan usage requires you to read a DTC (Diagnostic Trouble Code) *after* something breaks. This AI software runs constantly in the background, ingesting raw, real-time OBD2 telemetry (fuel trim, transmission temps, oil pressure fluctuations) via Bluetooth. It cross-references this micro-data against massive mechanical failure databases to predict a part failure (like an alternator bearing or a thermostat) weeks *before* it actually breaks, ensuring you never get stranded off-road.
