# Project: Oshun Cognition Protocol
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Education & Software

## Overview
A custom, AI-driven educational curriculum generator specifically designed for your granddaughter, Oshun. 

## The Concept
Standard schooling focuses on rote memorization. This software platform generates daily lateral-thinking puzzles, applied mechanics challenges, and emotional intelligence scenarios. It scales with her age, ensuring she learns *how* to think critically, solve real-world problems (like building basic 12V DC circuits or understanding companion planting), and manage emotional regulation, rather than just passing standardized tests.
