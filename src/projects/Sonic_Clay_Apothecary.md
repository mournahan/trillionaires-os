# Project: Sonic Clay Apothecary
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Luxury Wellness & Toiletries

## Concept
Merging John's proprietary French Green Clay and Xylitol tooth polish formulation with his acoustic biohacking practices. The product is a high-end, bespoke toiletry system designed for the luxury wellness market.

## The Hardware/Product Design
- **The Vessel**: The polish is stored in a heavy, obsidian-glass jar with a machined titanium lid.
- **The Induction Base**: The jar sits on a sleek, battery-powered base station (USB-C rechargeable).
- **Acoustic Therapy Integration**: When activated, the base station transmits specific ultrasonic/binaural frequencies (Alpha for morning relaxation, Theta for nighttime wind-down) directly through the glass and into the clay matrix. 
- **The Pitch**: While ultrasonic frequencies are proven in dental cleaning tools, applying acoustic resonance to the remineralization paste *before* application creates a unique, ultra-premium morning/nighttime ritual that aligns perfectly with the "Trillionaires" aesthetic.
