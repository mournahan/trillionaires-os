# Project: Gladiator Med-Evac Module
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Safety & Humanitarian

## Overview
A drop-in, composite bed module for the 2020 Jeep Gladiator Rubicon.

## The Concept
Converts the off-road vehicle into a ruggedized, mobile medical extraction unit for severe backcountry emergencies. Powered entirely by the Jeep's upgraded alternators and a dedicated 48V battery bank, the module is climate-controlled, sterile, and carries automated external defibrillators (AEDs) and trauma gear. It allows for the safe stabilization and extraction of injured hikers or off-roaders miles beyond where standard ambulances can reach.
