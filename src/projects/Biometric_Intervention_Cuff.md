# Project: Biometric Intervention Cuff
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Biohacking & Wearables

## The Problem with Current Trackers
Devices like Whoop, Oura, and Apple Watch suffer from "Open-Loop" architecture. They are incredibly good at telling you that your stress is high, your sleep is poor, or your HRV is tanking—but they do absolutely nothing to fix it. They are passive observers.

## The Trillionaires Solution: "Closed-Loop Intervention"
We build a device that doesn't just monitor the nervous system; it actively regulates it. 

### Hardware & Form Factor
- **Design:** Instead of a bulky watch, it is a sleek, obsidian-finished titanium **Ear Cuff** or a **Mastoid Patch** (sitting right behind the ear). It leans heavily into a premium, cyberpunk-jewelry aesthetic.
- **Sensing (The Input):** Uses optical PPG and micro-galvanic skin sensors to track Heart Rate Variability (HRV), vagal tone, and immediate stress spikes.
- **Actuation (The Output):** It features built-in **Bone-Conduction Transducers**.

### How It Works
If the cuff detects that your sympathetic nervous system is redlining (a sudden spike in stress, or failure to drop into deep sleep), it doesn't just log it on a dashboard. It instantly cross-references your bio-profile and actively transmits **Alpha or Theta binaural frequencies** via bone conduction directly into your inner ear. 

It forcefully down-regulates your nervous system in real-time, autonomously, without you having to open an app or put in headphones. Once your HRV stabilizes, it fades the audio out.
