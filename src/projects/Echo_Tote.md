# Project: Echo-Tote Logistics
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Logistics & Supply Chain

## The Problem: Single-Use Cold Chain Waste
Leveraging John and Dori's expertise at Pine Creek Pack: The global cold-chain and agricultural logistics network relies on millions of tons of single-use waxed cardboard, styrofoam, and disposable ice packs that choke landfills. 

## The Solution: Smart Reusable Containers
A standardized, vacuum-insulated, reusable carbon-composite tote designed to loop between agricultural producers (farms) and buyers (restaurants/grocery).
- **The Hardware**: Each tote features an embedded ESP32, an E-Ink display (replacing paper shipping labels), and an environmental datalogger. 
- **Functionality**: It tracks the internal temperature, shock/drops, and GPS location during transit via LoRaWAN/Cellular. If a pallet of organic produce is left on a hot loading dock, the E-Ink display flashes red and alerts the buyer *before* acceptance.
- **Impact**: Eliminates single-use packaging from the B2B supply chain while providing immutable data on food safety and handling.
