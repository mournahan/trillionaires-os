# Project: Micro-Factory (Piston Pump & Static Mixer)
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Manufacturing & Packaging

## Overview
A micro-manufacturing cell built around a precision piston pump and static mixer. This setup allows for the perfect homogenization of high-viscosity, two-part materials at the exact moment of dispensing.

## Active Applications

### 1. Electronics Encapsulation (Potting)
- **Target**: The Holdover Sentinel, ESP32 Irrigation Nodes, and any outdoor hardware.
- **Process**: Using the static mixer to inject 2-part liquid silicone or epoxy directly into the hardware enclosures. It cures into a solid block, making the electronics 100% waterproof, shockproof, and vibration-proof (military-grade weatherproofing).

### 2. Bi-Phase Cosmetics (Trillionaires LLC)
- **Target**: Luxury skincare and dental polish serums.
- **Process**: Keeping active, degradable ingredients (like enzymes or specific probiotics) in a separate hopper from the liquid base. The static mixer homogenizes them straight into the final glass retail bottle on the assembly line, ensuring maximum "fresh-activated" potency for the customer.

### 3. Food-Grade Silicone Tooling
- **Target**: Trillionaires LLC high-end DIY culinary kits.
- **Process**: Dispensing 2-part Platinum Silicone over 3D-printed master positives to rapidly mass-produce custom molds (for chocolate, tofu, butter, etc.) without the risk of air bubbles or inconsistent hand-mixing.
