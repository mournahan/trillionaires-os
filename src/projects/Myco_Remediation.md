# Project: Myco-Remediation Mat
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Logistics & Ecology

## Overview
Biodegradable shipping pallets and packaging mats made from compressed mycelium and dormant seeds.

## The Concept
Tying into Pine Creek Pack logistics and ecological restoration. Instead of wooden pallets or styrofoam mats that end up in landfills, these are grown from tough fungal mycelium embedded with white clover seeds. Once they reach the end of their logistical lifecycle, they can be crushed and left in degraded soil or burn scars. As they break down, the fungi remediate toxins from the soil, and the clover seeds sprout to fix nitrogen, turning supply chain waste into ecological fertilizer.
