# Project: Obsidian Avalanche Beacon
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Safety & Humanitarian

## The Problem
Standard avalanche beacons only emit a localized radio frequency. If Search and Rescue is not within immediate range, the victim is lost.

## The Solution
An ultra-premium, wearable survival beacon utilizing the Iridium RockBLOCK satellite network.
- Triggers automatically upon detecting the extreme G-force and biometric shock of an avalanche or fall.
- Instantly transmits exact GPS coordinates and the wearer's current heart rate to a global rescue network.
- **Impact**: Turns a localized search into an exact, pinpointed rescue operation instantly, saving lives in extreme backcountry scenarios.
