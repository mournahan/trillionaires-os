# Project: Kestrel Biotelemetry Rig
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Falconry & Ecology

## Overview
A miniaturized, ultra-lightweight telemetry backpack designed for the rehabilitation and training of American Kestrels.

## The Concept
Current falconry trackers only provide GPS. This micro-rig (weighing less than 2 grams) includes a LoRaWAN transmitter and a micro-optical heart rate sensor. It provides real-time GPS tracking while simultaneously monitoring the raptor's cardiovascular stress levels during rehabilitation flights, ensuring the bird is not over-exerted before release into the wild.
