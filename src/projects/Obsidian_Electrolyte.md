# Project: Obsidian Electrolyte Forge
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Luxury Wellness

## Overview
An automated, countertop mineral dosing system for bespoke hydration.

## The Concept
Instead of buying generic electrolyte powders filled with sugar, this high-end appliance syncs with your biometric sweat-tracking data. It uses precision peristaltic pumps (from our Micro-Factory designs) to automatically formulate a bespoke, perfectly balanced remineralization drink into your glass. It utilizes liquid magnesium (Epsom salt derivatives) and trace minerals from French Green Clay, tailored exactly to what your body depleted that day.
