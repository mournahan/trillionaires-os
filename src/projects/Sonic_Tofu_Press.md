# Project: Trillionaires Sonic-Aged Tofu Press
**Status**: Ideation
**Category**: Luxury Culinary

## Overview
A high-end, automated tofu press that combines chemical and acoustic processing.

## The Concept
Catering to John's specific dietary preference for scratching-cooking soy milk and curdling with Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate). This obsidian-finished press doesn't just squeeze out water; it utilizes localized low-frequency acoustic vibration (binaural frequencies) during the pressing phase. This acoustic aging process slightly alters the protein matrix, resulting in a perfectly dense, artisanal texture that maximizes the surface area for later probiotic fermentation (like yogurt or sauerkraut marinades).
